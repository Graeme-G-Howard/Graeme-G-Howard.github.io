Gorilla Gaming Promotional Imagery
I undertook an exciting project to refine and elevate the visual representation of Gorilla Gaming's diverse range of products. I worked on a variety of gaming gear, like controllers and custom arcade sticks.

With gaming enthusiasts as the target audience, I focused on infusing the imagery with energy and excitement. This involved enhancing colors to make them pop, fine-tuning lighting to create depth and dimension, and ensuring that every angle showcased the product's features in the best possible light.

Working closely with the Gorilla Gaming team, I aimed to align the promotional imagery with their brand identity while emphasizing the key features of each product. Through creative design choices and strategic placement of branding elements, I captured the attention of gamers and drove interest in Gorilla Gaming's offerings, ultimately enhancing their presence in the gaming market and resonating with their target audience.
