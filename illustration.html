Original Illustrations
I have a passion for illustration and typography. My influences include graffiti, street art, tattoos, heavy metal album covers and classic 2D animation. I like artwork with strong outlines and a bold style. I especially love crafting original typography where I continue to expand my own unique style.

sawone typography & illustration

This piece draws inspiration from graffiti and tattoo art, featuring hand-drawn typography and illustrations. I started by researching traditional tattoo designs and blackletter fonts and gathered reference images for elements like flowers and hairstyles.

I began with sketches, trying out different ideas until I settled on a final design. Using Photoshop, I combined these sketches to find the right look.

Then, I moved to Illustrator to create the final artwork. I traced over the sketches to make crisp lines and added color and shading. I used halftone dots to give a nod to traditional tattoo shading techniques.

The result is a blend of urban style and digital precision, bringing together classic influences in a modern design.

Puck Prayers: Ice Hockey-Inspired Artwork

Inspired by tattoo flash, this irreverent twist on the traditional 'praying hands' caters to ice hockey fans. Hand-drawn and digitally recreated in Illustrator, the design is optimised for screen printing with a limited colour palette and half-tone shading, perfect for merchandise like apparel and stickers.

I used an AI text-to-image generator to create the initial pose reference images of hockey gloves in the praying hands position, as I wasn’t able to source photography of the equipment in the correct position. These reference images weren’t perfect, sometimes having many more fingers than needed as is common with AI-generated images, however it gave me a good starting point to work with.

I then sketched out my own version of the design, adding extra elements such as the rosary beads, flowing sleeves and light rays.

Finally, I digitised the sketch and added text and colour in Illustrator.
