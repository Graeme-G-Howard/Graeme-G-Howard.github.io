Promise of Bloodshed Album Art
As a part of the New Zealand Hardcore and Metal music scenes, I have designed many album covers and t-shirts for local bands. My most recent project was the album art for the upcoming re-release of Promise of Bloodshed's Hatred Inherit.

Hatred Inherit

The dark and gritty artwork reflects the themes of the heavy, metalcore music.

One of the challenges involved in this project involved maintaining the legibility of small text on a dark, textured background. I solved this problem using high contrast and careful use of shadows.
