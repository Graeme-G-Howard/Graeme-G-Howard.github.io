About Me

I'm a graphic designer who thrives on blending creativity with technical skills to craft visually stunning designs. My design journey began with a fascination for album covers on vinyl records. Each cover told a story before you even listened to the music, igniting my passion for the art of visual storytelling.

Drawing inspiration from various sources such as anime, street art, tattoo art, as well as traditional design styles, I bring a diverse perspective to my work. I enjoy tackling technical challenges hands-on and take pride in keeping files organized for seamless collaboration. While I value teamwork and communication, I also appreciate the focus that comes with putting on my headphones and diving deep into a project.

I have a preference for working with established brands, finding that working within existing brand constraints allows me to unleash my creativity and discover innovative solutions to design challenges.

Outside of the design world, you'll find me hanging out with my dog, Archer, who fills my life with joy and keeps me active. In my free time, I immerse myself in the worlds of ARPGs and competitive fighting games, where quick thinking and strategy are essential.
